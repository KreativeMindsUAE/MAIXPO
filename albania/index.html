About MAIXPO Albania

A focused AI marketing event
for the Balkan region.

MAIXPO Albania is the AI and Marketing Conference & Expo for the Balkan region. It brings marketing leaders, agency teams and brand owners together to learn practical AI for campaigns, content, paid media and analytics.

This edition is planned for a smaller, more focused audience than Kuala Lumpur. We would rather build a room of people who can act on what they learn than chase a large headline number.

Attendance targets and the full speaker list will be published once the venue is confirmed.

  • First MAIXPO edition in Tirana
  • Built for Albania and neighbouring Balkan markets
  • Same practical, tactics-first approach as Kuala Lumpur
  • Local speakers and local business context
